Terms of Service, HIPAA Compliance & Clinical Decision Support Notice

                    
Regulatory framework, data protection standards, and physician responsibility guidelines.

                

                
            


            

                
                

                    

                        
                        
1. Clinical Decision Support (CDS) & Non-Diagnostic Scope

                    

                    
CarePolicyGuard is an administrative assistive platform engineered specifically to verify prior-authorization alignment against published Medicare National Coverage Determinations (NCDs) and Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs). CarePolicyGuard does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, disease management, or clinical prescriptions.

                    
All coverage determinations generated are purely advisory. Attending physicians and healthcare practitioners retain sole legal and medical authority for patient evaluations, clinical judgment, and insurance pre-authorization submissions.

                


                
                

                    

                        
                        
2. HIPAA Safe Harbor De-Identification (45 CFR § 164.514(b))

                    

                    
In full compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), CarePolicyGuard sanitizes direct and quasi-identifiers prior to algorithmic adjudication. The platform strips Medicare Beneficiary Identifiers (MBIs), Social Security Numbers (SSNs), patient full names, full dates of birth, street addresses, and contact numbers.

                    
Only essential clinical parameters (e.g., LVEF, NYHA functional class, diagnostic sleep study indices, medical therapy durations) are processed to determine coverage policy compliance.

                


                
                

                    

                        
                        
3. Data Minimization & Ephemeral Zero-Retention Policy

                    

                    
Under the HIPAA Security Rule principle of Minimum Necessary handling: Any clinical records or patient notes that do not require mandatory saving are never saved.

                    
    
                        
  • In-Memory Processing: Interactive policy verifications operate ephemerally in volatile memory. If an evaluation is not explicitly finalized or submitted, no medical documentation is persisted to disk.
    • 
                        
  • Right to Erasure (Audit Purge): Authorized users can permanently purge unsubmitted test records and audit entries at any time using the one-click purge action in the Audit Ledger.
    • 
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        
                        
4. Cryptographic Proof & Chain-of-Custody (SHA-256)

                    

                    
When an adjudication is recorded into the audit trail, an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic seal is generated binding the timestamp, de-identified patient criteria, exact CMS clause citations, and calibrated confidence score. This prevents retroactive tampering and ensures non-repudiation for CMS administrative audits.

                


                
                

                    

                        
                        
5. Clinician Attestation & Statutory Compliance

                    

                    
By using CarePolicyGuard to assemble pre-authorization packets, the user certifies that all uploaded documents reflect accurate medical records and authentic clinical observations. Users acknowledge awareness of the Federal False Claims Act (31 U.S.C. §§ 3729–3733) and agree that final insurance claim submissions are certified under penalty of law.
